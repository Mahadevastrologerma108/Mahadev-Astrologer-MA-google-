Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the daily horoscope 100% accurate?

No, the daily horoscope is a general calculation based on transits. For 100% accuracy, analyzing a personal Birth Chart is essential.

What if the horoscope predictions do not match?

If the results do not align, the 'Mahadasha' in your chart might be more dominant. In such cases, a complete horoscope or palmistry analysis is recommended.

Birth Chart or Palmistry: Which is better?

If the exact birth time is known, the Birth Chart is superior. Otherwise, Palmistry becomes the medium for accurate guidance.
