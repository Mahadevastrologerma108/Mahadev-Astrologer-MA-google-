Vastu Shastra Consultation FAQ

Kya bina tod-fod ke Vastu dosh theek ho sakta hai?

Haan, bilkul! Panchtattva santulan aur urja siddhant ke anusar, sahi disha mein uchit dhaatu (metal tapes) ya rangon ka upyog karke, urja ko bina kisi tod-fod ke purna roop se santulit kiya ja sakta hai.

Ghar mein sabse bada Vastu dosh kya hota hai?

Ghar ke Ishan Kon (North-East) mein shauchalaya ya kitchen hona, aur Nairutya Kon (South-West) mein mukhya dwar ya gaddha hona sabse hanikarak Vastu dosh mana jata hai, jo unnati ko badhit karta hai.

Vastu audit ke liye mujhe kya taiyari karni hogi?

Aapko keval apne bhavan ka ek saaf naxsha (floor plan) aur mukhya dwar ki sateek compass degree taiyar rakhni hogi. Isse hum 16-zone grid lagakar sateek vishleshan kar sakte hain.

Metal Tapes aur Salts kaise karya karte hain?

Vibhinn dishaon mein asantulit tatvon (jaise jal kshetr mein agni ka hona) ko rokne (block) karne ke liye Metal Tapes ka prayog hota hai. Saath hi, vishesh Salts (namak) nakaratmak urja ko sokh kar vatavaran ko shuddh karte hain.

Mirror (Darpan) ka galat sthan kya prabhav dalta hai?

Mirror jal tatva aur aakash (space) ka pratinidhitv karta hai. Ise dakshin ya pashchim disha mein lagane se agni aur prithvi tatva asantulit ho jate hain, jisse vyapar mein hani aur swasthya par pratikool prabhav padta hai.
