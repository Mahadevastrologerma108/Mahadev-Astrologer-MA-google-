Aaj Ka Rashifal

Sitaron ki chaal aur aapka bhavishya

Mesh (Aries)

Aaj aapka atmavishwas badhega. Naye kaam ki shuruat ke liye din shubh hai.

Vrishabh (Taurus)

Aarthik mamlo mein savdhani rakhein. Pariwar ka pura sahyog milega.

Mithun (Gemini)

Doston ke saath samay bitega. Career mein tarakki ke naye avsar milenge.

Kark (Cancer)

Swasthya ka dhyan rakhein. Man mein shanti bani rahegi.

Singh (Leo)

Samaj mein maan-samman badhega. Koi ruka hua kaam pura hoga.

Kanya (Virgo)

Mehnat ka fal milega. Vyapar mein labh ke yog ban rahe hain.

Tula (Libra)

Rishton mein madhurta aayegi. Aaj aap bahut srijanatmak rahenge.

Vrishchik (Scorpio)

Fisul-kharchi se bachein. Krodh par niyantran rakhna zaroori hai.

Dhanu (Sagittarius)

Yatra ke yog hain. Dharmik kaaryon mein ruchi badhegi.

Makar (Capricorn)

Career mein naye badlav aayenge. Senior ka support milega.

Kumbh (Aquarius)

Samaajik daira badhega. Naye rishte banne ke yog hain.

Meen (Pisces)

Aaj aapka din bhagyashali rahega. Sabhi adhure kaam pure honge.
