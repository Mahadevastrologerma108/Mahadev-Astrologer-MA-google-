Kya in kits ke yantra purv-urjit (pre-energized) aate hain?

Haan, aapke pas bhejne se purva pratyek yantra aur samagri ki Pran Pratishtha vaidik mantron dwara ki jati hai taaki aapko inka purna labh prapt ho.
