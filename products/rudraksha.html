Vedic Rules for Wearing Rudraksha

Rudraksha is extremely sacred. To get its full benefits, it is mandatory to follow these rules:

1. Purification & Auspicious Timing

Rudraksha should always be worn on Monday, Shivratri, or any auspicious time only after purifying it with Gangajal and Panchamrit.

2. Maintaining Purity

The Rudraksha should be removed and kept in a pure place while consuming meat or alcohol, attending a funeral, or sleeping.

3. Daily Mantra Chanting

The person wearing the Rudraksha must chant 'Om Namah Shivaya' or the respective Beej Mantra at least 108 times daily.
