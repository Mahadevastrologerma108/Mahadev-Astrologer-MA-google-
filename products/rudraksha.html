Rudraksha FAQs & Guidelines

Asli Rudraksha ki pehchan kaise hoti hai?

Asli Rudraksha ke bheetar utne hi beej (seeds) ya kaksh (compartments) hote hain, jitne uske bahar mukh hote hain. Iski sateek pushti keval laboratory mein X-Ray parikshan dwara hi ki ja sakti hai.

Kya ise dharan karne ke koi niyam hain?

Haan, purna adhyatmik labh hetu ise rati (raat) mein sote samay, shav yatra (funeral) mein, aur ashuddh sthanon par utar dena uchit mana jata hai. Pratah snan ke pashchat ise punah dharan karein.

Rudraksha ko urjit (energize) kaise kiya jata hai?

Bhejne se purva iska panchamrit (doodh, dahi, ghee, shahad, gangajal) se abhishek kiya jata hai. Tatpashchat, 'Om Namah Shivaya' aur nishchit mukhi ke beej mantra ka jaap karke iski Pran Pratishtha ki jati hai.
