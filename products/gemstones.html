Gemstone Guidelines & FAQs

Mujhe kaun sa ratna dharan karna chahiye?

Ratna sadaiv apni janm-kundali ke Lagna aur Trikon (5ve aur 9ve bhav) ke swami ke anusar dharan karna uchit hota hai. Bina kundali vishleshan ke ratna dharan karna hanikarak ho sakta hai.

Ratna ko jagrit (energize) kaise kiya jata hai?

Aapke pas bhejne se purva, ratna ko Ganga jal aur kachhe doodh mein shuddh kiya jata hai. Tatpashchat, aapke naam aur gotra se nishchit grah ke vaidik mantron ka jaap karke iski Pran Pratishtha ki jati hai.

Heated aur Unheated ratna mein kya bhed hai?

Heated ratna ko tapman badhakar rangin banaya jata hai, jisse uski prakritik urja nasht ho jati hai. Jyotishiye labh ke liye sadaiv unheated (prakritik) ratna hi dharan karna aavashyak hai, jo hum pradan karte hain.
