Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

At Mahadev Astrologer MA, we take your privacy as a divine responsibility. This policy outlines how we handle your personal and spiritual data.

1. Client UID & Data Consent

We do not store your personal birth details (Date, Time, Place) unless you explicitly give us permission. If you choose to generate a "Client UID" by ticking the consent box in our forms, your details are saved securely for future consultations. Without this consent, you are treated as a 'One-Time Client', and your data is cleared after the session.

2. Choice of Communication

Your comfort is our priority. We offer three distinct modes of contact: WhatsApp, Telegram, and Email. We will only reach out to you through the specific platform you have selected during the form submission.

3. Confidentiality of Consultations

Every consultation, whether it relates to Astrology, Ayurveda, or Sound Healing, is kept 100% confidential. We never share your life problems or birth charts with any third party. Your secrets remain between you and the divine guidance.

4. Cookies & Google AdSense

We use third-party vendors, including Google, which use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your interests. You may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Testimonial Policy

We value your feedback, but we value your privacy more. Testimonials are only displayed on our website if you provide explicit public consent. If no such permission is given, your identity and feedback remain strictly private.

By using our website, you hereby consent to this Privacy Policy.
