Privacy Policy

1. Authentication & Google Login

To provide a personalized experience, MAHADEV ASTROLOGER MA offers Google Login. When you sign in, we only store your basic profile details (Name, Email, and UID) to identify you. This process is handled securely through Firebase Authentication.

2. User Choice & Divine UID Policy

We respect your right to privacy and offer complete User Choice regarding your data:

* No Forced Storage: We do not force any user to save their data permanently. Providing a permanent record is entirely optional.
* Temporary by Default: Unless you explicitly check the "Divine UID" box in our forms, any sensitive birth or family details provided are treated as Temporary. This data is used only to complete your current request and is not stored in our long-term active records.
* Permanent on Request: Only when a user chooses to "Opt-In" for a UID, we retain the information for future consultations and record-keeping. Your privacy is in your hands.

We use Google AdSense as a third-party vendor to serve advertisements. Google uses the DART cookie to serve ads based on your interests and visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.

You can opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy here: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security & Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase, ensuring high-level encryption. We respect your User Choice and rights: you can request access to your data, ask for corrections, or request complete deletion of your records at any time. We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party marketing companies.
