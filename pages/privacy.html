Privacy Policy

At Mahadev Astrologer MA, we prioritize your privacy above all else. Humara system aapki privacy ko dhyan mein rakh kar design kiya gaya hai.

1. No Data Storage Policy

Hum kisi bhi user ka personal data (Name, DOB, Time) apne server par store nahi karte hain. Aap jo bhi details form mein bharte hain, wo seedhe WhatsApp ke madhyam se hamare paas pahunchti hai.

2. Client Permission (Optional)

Agar aap chahte hain ki future guidance ke liye aapko baar-baar details na deni padein, toh aap humein apni details save karne ki permission de sakte hain. Ye poori tarah aapki marzi par nirbhar hai.

3. Data Security

Aapki aur hamari baatchit end-to-end encrypted rehti hai. Hum aapka contact number ya details kisi bhi third-party ko kabhi nahi bechte.

By ticking the consent box on our forms, you agree to our privacy handling process.
