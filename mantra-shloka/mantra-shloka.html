🐘 Pratham Pujya: Lord Ganesha

Vakratunda Mahakaya Mantra

"Vakratunda Mahakaya Suryakoti Samaprabha,
Nirvighnam Kuru Me Deva Sarva-Kaaryeshu Sarvada."

Meaning: O Lord with a curved trunk and a mighty body, whose brilliance equals a million suns. Please make all my endeavors free of obstacles, always.

🔱 The Supreme: Lord Shiva (Mahadev)

1. Panchakshari Mantra

"Om Namah Shivaya"

Meaning: I bow to Lord Shiva. It is the core mantra for inner peace and destroying negative energies.

2. Mahamrityunjaya Mantra

"Om Tryambakam Yajamahe Sugandhim Pushtivardhanam,
Urvarukamiva Bandhanan Mrityor Mukshiya Maamritat."

Meaning: We worship the three-eyed Lord (Shiva) who is fragrant and nourishes all beings. May He liberate us from death for the sake of immortality, just as a cucumber is severed from its bondage to the creeper.

🪐 Navagraha & Surya Dev

Navagraha Peeda Hara Stotram

"Brahma Murari Tripurantakari Bhanuh Shashi Bhumisutaro Budhashcha,
Gurushcha Shukrah Shani Rahu Ketavah Kurvantu Sarve Mama Suprabhatam."

Meaning: May Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, and Ketu—may all these divine beings make my morning (my life) auspicious and peaceful.

2. Om Ravaye Namah
3. Om Suryaya Namah

5. Om Khagaya Namah

🌸 Lord Vishnu & Divine Goddesses

Lord Vishnu Mantra

"Shaantaakaaram Bhujagashayanam Padmanaabham Suresham,
Vishwaadhaaram Gaganasadrisham Meghavarnam Shubhaangam."

Meaning: I bow to Lord Vishnu, who is the embodiment of peace, resting on the serpent bed, having a lotus navel, the Lord of Gods, the support of the universe.

Goddess Laxmi Mantra

"Om Shreem Hreem Shreem Kamale Kamalalaye Praseed Praseed,
Om Shreem Hreem Shreem Mahalakshmaye Namah."

Meaning: O Goddess of wealth, who resides in the lotus, please be pleased with me and bless me with abundance and prosperity.

Goddess Durga Mantra

"Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu Shakti Rupena Samsthita,
Namastasyai Namastasyai Namastasyai Namo Namah."

Meaning: Salutations to the Goddess who resides in all beings in the form of Shakti (Power). I bow to you again and again.

Goddess Saraswati Mantra

"Saraswati Namastubhyam Varade Kamarupini,
Vidyarambham Karishyami Siddhirbhavatu Me Sada."

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Saraswati, the giver of boons. I am beginning my studies, may I always attain success.
