Special Information Regarding Panchang and Muhurat (FAQ)

Why is there a time difference according to location?

Panchang calculation is based on Sunrise. Since the sunrise time varies in every city, there can be a difference of a few minutes in the timings of Rahukaal and Shubh Muhurat. The calculations provided here are based on a standard time.

What is the significance of Abhijit Muhurat?

According to scriptures, Abhijit Muhurat is considered the most auspicious time of the day. Any work started during this time is successful with the blessings of Mahadev. If you cannot find a specific muhurat, you can use this time.
